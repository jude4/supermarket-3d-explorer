Original assets authored for Supermarket Explorer.

models/empty-gondola.glb — Blender-modelled reusable empty fixture geometry.
textures/limestone-tile.png — procedural tiled floor material.
textures/fixture-shadow.png — procedural contact shading.
audio/door-slide.wav, footstep.wav, ventilation.wav — original synthesized PCM.
products/packaging-atlas.png — original front/back fictional packaging.
products/price-atlas.png — catalogue-driven promotional prices.
products/images/*.png — original catalogue artwork.
products/inspection/*.png — original higher-resolution inspection labels.

Product 3D models are generated by src/products/geometry.ts; placement is configured
in src/products/placement.ts. scripts/generate-retail.py rebuilds packaging from
src/catalogue/products.json and retail-config.json. No third-party retail brands,
commercial packaging, downloaded stock models or external asset URLs are used.
Short shopping/scanner feedback is synthesized locally with Web Audio.
